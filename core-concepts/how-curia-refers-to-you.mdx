Curia writes to your colleagues, clients and contacts in your name. When it mentions you in the third person, it needs to know what to call you and which pronouns to use. If it has nothing to go on, it does not guess from your name. It uses your name or "they", and it does the same for any other contact whose pronouns it has not been told.

## What Curia knows

Every agent sees these details about you, taken from your contact record:

| Detail | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Always shown. |
| **Preferred name** | The short form you go by, such as "Avy" for Avery. |
| **Pronouns** | Used when Curia writes about you. Unset means name or "they". |
| **Title** | Used in introductions and signatures. |

## Setting them

**In chat.** Say it once: "I go by Avy", "my pronouns are she/her", "my title is CEO". Curia saves it to your contact and applies it from your next message.

**During setup.** The setup assistant asks for all three in your first conversation. It counts as done once your pronouns are set.

**In the console.** Open **Contacts**, pick yourself, and edit the preferred name, pronouns and title fields.

## Changing them later

Tell Curia the new value in chat, or edit your contact in the console. To clear a value, ask Curia to remove it. Pronouns then go back to the default of your name or "they".
