Curia is an AI executive office for one executive, the **principal**. A team of specialized agents reads email, manages the calendar, keeps track of people and decisions, does research, and replies on the principal's behalf by email, Signal, SMS, Slack, voice, and the web console. Curia runs on your own server, keeps a persistent knowledge graph of the principal's world, and records every action in an audit trail.

## Why Curia

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your data stays on your server">
    Curia is single-tenant and self-hosted. Emails, contacts, and the knowledge graph are stored on your infrastructure. The model and speech providers you configure process the content they're sent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Every action is auditable">
    An append-only audit log traces every event — every message received, tool invoked, and decision made — with full causal chains.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Governance by architecture">
    Security boundaries are enforced at the framework level, not by policy. A compromised email adapter cannot invoke tools or write to memory — it is architecturally prevented.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Memory that lasts">
    Curia remembers across restarts. The knowledge graph stores people, decisions, and facts with confidence scores that age gracefully over time.
  </Step>
</Steps>
