After your first conversation, Curia keeps learning in short sessions with you. It books each next session with you at the end of the last one. It never books one you didn't agree to, and never more than one at a time.

## How sessions are booked

- **On your calendar.** Once your calendar is connected, a session is a busy event with only you invited.
- **Before that,** Curia remembers the time and reminds you shortly before.

If you move the event, Curia follows it. If you cancel it, Curia asks once when suits instead. If you don't turn up, it sends one "missed you, when suits better?" and books your answer.

A session works by chat or by voice, like your first conversation. On a call, Curia comes with an agenda ready.

## Check-ins

A check-in looks back at how the last stretch went:

- **What landed.** Curia summarises what it did since you last spoke, and asks what it got right and what annoyed you.
- **What arrives too often,** or not often enough.
- **Corrections.** What Curia should stop doing, or do differently. It applies each one as soon as you confirm it, and reads it back.
- **When to talk next.**

## Choosing a rhythm

At the end of onboarding, Curia proposes a regular rhythm, and you decide:

- **Weekly,** at a time that suits you.
- **Another cadence,** such as fortnightly or monthly.
- **None.** Curia only gets in touch when something needs you.

This is the last onboarding topic: once it and the review are done, onboarding is finished. You can change the rhythm any time by telling Curia.

## The weekly session

If you choose a regular session, Curia prepares an agenda before each one from what happened that week:

- **Independence.** Whether the level you chose still fits. Curia recalls what it did under it, and what it held back on that it could have just done.
- **What's new.** New or changed features that affect you, and whether to switch them on. Anything Curia does now that you'd rather it stopped.
- **New plays.** What took your time this week that Curia could take on, and any recurring task you'd like it to own.
- **Priorities.** Your top three things this week and this month, and who or what to watch for. Curia records any changes in your priorities.

## Changing things between sessions

You don't have to wait for a session. Tell Curia whenever something should change: "be more independent with scheduling", "stop sending me a daily recap", "check my work email every 30 minutes". It applies the change and reads it back.
